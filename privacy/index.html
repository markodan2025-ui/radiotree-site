Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

RadioTree does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no login, and no tracking of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. RadioTree contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking software. We have no servers that receive data from the app and no way to identify you.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and listening data stay on your television and never leave it:

  • Favourite and recently played stations
  • Custom stations you add yourself
  • Interface preferences, such as theme, font, and screensaver settings
  • Cached stream addresses, so stations start faster

Uninstalling RadioTree removes all of it permanently.

Connections the app makes

To play radio, RadioTree has to reach the internet. Two kinds of connection happen, both directly between your device and the other party:

  • Radio stream servers. When you play a station, your device connects to that broadcaster's server. The broadcaster receives your IP address, the same as when you open their website in a browser. Each broadcaster handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Radio-Browser. An open community directory used to search stations and find the best available stream quality. Search terms and your IP address reach that service. See radio-browser.info.

We receive nothing from either.

Permissions

RadioTree requests internet access only. It does not use a microphone, camera, location, contacts, or files.

Children

RadioTree is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with an updated date. Should RadioTree ever begin collecting data, we will say so here before that version is released.
